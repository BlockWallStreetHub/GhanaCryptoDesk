Ghana & Africa Bitcoin News

Regulation, adoption, community and scams — everything touching Bitcoin, blockchain and crypto in Ghana and across Africa. Live headlines below, plus our timeline of the rules that now govern crypto in Ghana.

Ghana's crypto-law timeline

10 JUL 2025
Registration order. The Bank of Ghana directs all virtual asset service providers to register by 15 August 2025, covering local and foreign platforms serving Ghanaians. Citi Newsroom
1 OCT 2025
Draft bill complete. The central bank confirms it has finished drafting the VASP Bill with the SEC and other regulators. MyJoyOnline
5 NOV 2025
Policy position. The Bank of Ghana reports more than 100 registered VASPs, plans a Virtual Assets Regulatory Office, and reaffirms virtual assets are not legal tender. Graphic Online
19 DEC 2025
Parliament passes the VASP Act. Banks are barred from holding or trading crypto directly and become intermediaries to licensed providers. NewsGhana
29 DEC 2025
President signs it into law as Act 1154, with dual oversight by the Bank of Ghana and SEC. MyJoyOnline
FEB 2026
Sandbox and advertising ban. The SEC publishes sandbox guidelines and admits six entities to a one-year sandbox; the Bank of Ghana and SEC order providers to halt public advertising until rules are issued. Mariblock
MAY 2026
Framework rolled out. Licensing requirements and supervisory guidelines continue to be developed under the new Act. Ghana News Agency
20 MAY 2026
Africa Bitcoin Day opens in Accra, the first stop on an eight-city, ten-day continental tour. MyJoyOnline
JUL 2026
Implementation under way. Official materials show registration, licensing, supervision and sandbox measures operating under Act 1154. CryptoSlate
