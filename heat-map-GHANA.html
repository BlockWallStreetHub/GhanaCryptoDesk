How to read it: each cell is the change in the price of 1 Bitcoin measured in that currency. Green = Bitcoin got more expensive in that currency (Bitcoin rose, or the currency weakened). Red = cheaper. When the same Bitcoin move shows different colours across currencies, the difference is the currency itself — which is why many Africans see Bitcoin as protection against a weakening local money.
Each cell shows Bitcoin’s % change in US dollars from the first day of that month to the first day of the next, using rounded first-of-month prices (so values are approximate). The final row averages completed months. The current month (marked *) is measured to today’s live price. Notice how many red months — even in winning years.
