Bitcoin in Ghana — at a glance

It now has a law

Parliament passed the Virtual Asset Service Providers (VASP) Act on 19 December 2025, and the President signed it on 29 December 2025 (Act 1154). The Bank of Ghana and the Securities and Exchange Commission share licensing and supervision.
Sources: NewsGhana · MyJoyOnline

Millions already use it

The Bank of Ghana said more than three million Ghanaians use virtual assets, with an estimated 100 service providers operating. Bitcoin is not legal tender — the cedi remains the only legal tender.
Sources: Citi Newsroom · Graphic Online

Top 5 in Africa by volume

Chainalysis ranks Ghana #40 of 151 countries on its 2025 adoption index, with roughly $11 billion in crypto value received between July 2024 and June 2025 — fifth-largest in Africa.
See the heat map · Sources: African Exponent · MEXC News

Rules are still being finished

In February 2026 the Bank of Ghana and SEC ordered providers to stop advertising to the public while detailed rules were pending, and banks may not hold or trade crypto directly. Always check that a platform is registered or licensed before you send money.
Sources: Mariblock · Bank of Ghana

Start here: four steps

Learn before you buy. Understand what Bitcoin is and why we focus on accumulating it slowly, not trading. Our Block Wall Street courses are built for beginners.
Pick a registered on-ramp. Compare options on the Exchanges & Merchants page, then verify the company with the Bank of Ghana virtual assets desk before depositing.
Start small and stay steady. Try the DCA calculator: a fixed cedi amount every week or month smooths out the ups and downs.
Take custody. When your stack grows, move it to a wallet you control. Write the recovery words on paper, keep them offline, and never share them with anyone — ever.
Stay safe. Nobody can guarantee Bitcoin returns — anyone who does is scamming you. Never share your password, OTP or recovery words. On peer-to-peer (P2P) platforms, only release Bitcoin after the money shows in your own Mobile Money or bank account, and only accept payment from an account in the buyer's own verified name. Never borrow money to buy Bitcoin.
