Add your public wallet addresses on Bitcoin, EVM chains (Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon, BNB Chain, Avalanche) and Solana, plus anything you hold on an exchange, and see everything in one place — valued in dollars or cedis, with your Bitcoin share front and centre.

View-only. Never enter a recovery phrase or private key here — or anywhere. This tool only needs your public address (the one you share to receive money). Your list is saved only in this browser. To read balances, your browser asks public services (mempool.space, public blockchain nodes, CoinGecko) about your address, so they can see the address and your internet connection — treat your addresses as visible to them.
